Make a practical coaster while learning how evenly spaced increases turn a small ring into a flat circle. This is demo editorial content awaiting a human maker's size and stitch-count review. Instructions use US crochet terms.

## At a glance
- Skill level: beginner
- Finished size: approximately 9 to 10 cm across, depending on yarn and gauge
- Abbreviations: sc = single crochet, sl st = slip stitch

## Materials
- Approximately 20 g of medium-weight cotton yarn in cream
- Small amount of sage cotton yarn for the border

## Tools
- 4 mm crochet hook, or a size that makes a firm fabric
- Stitch marker
- Tapestry needle
- Scissors

## Instructions
### 1. Make the center
With cream yarn, make a magic ring and work 6 sc into it. Pull the ring closed. Place a marker in the first stitch and work in a continuous spiral.

### 2. Increase to form a circle
Round 2: work 2 sc in each stitch (12 stitches). Round 3: repeat 1 sc, then 2 sc in the next stitch, six times (18 stitches). Round 4: repeat 2 sc, then 2 sc in the next stitch, six times (24 stitches).

### 3. Continue the circle
Round 5: repeat 3 sc, then 2 sc in the next stitch, six times (30 stitches). Round 6: repeat 4 sc, then 2 sc in the next stitch, six times (36 stitches). If the coaster is smaller than your cup base, continue the same increase pattern for another round.

### 4. Add the edge
Join sage yarn. Work 1 sc in each stitch around, then join with a sl st. Fasten off and weave in the tails.

### 5. Set the shape
Dampen the coaster lightly and dry it flat. Check that a cup sits steadily on top before using it.

## Tips
- Cotton is a useful choice for absorbing small drips from a drink.
- Move the marker up at the start of every round so you do not lose your count.
- If the edge starts to wave, check for extra increases before adding more rounds.

## Common mistakes
- Forgetting the increase spacing makes the circle curl or ruffle. Count at the end of each round.
- A very loose fabric may not support a cup well. Use a smaller hook if needed.

## FAQ
### Can I make a set of four?
Yes. Weigh or measure yarn before starting so the coasters can match.

### Can I machine wash it?
Follow the yarn label and dry flat to help preserve the circular shape.
