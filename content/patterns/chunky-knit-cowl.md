A relaxed weekend project with a soft drape and a forgiving rib texture. This is an editorial draft awaiting gauge and fit review.

## At a glance
- Skill level: beginner
- Construction: knit flat, then seamed
- Finished size: approximately 66 cm around

## Materials
- Approximately 180 m of bulky weight yarn
- Matching yarn for seaming

## Tools
- 8 mm knitting needles
- Tapestry needle
- Scissors

## Instructions
### 1. Cast on
Cast on 28 stitches with a relaxed edge. Leave a long tail for seaming.

### 2. Work the texture
Row 1: knit two, purl two to the end. Row 2: work each stitch as it appears. Repeat these rows until the piece measures approximately 66 cm, or your preferred circumference.

### 3. Bind off and join
Bind off loosely in pattern. Lay the strip flat without twisting, bring short ends together, and seam with mattress stitch.

### 4. Finish
Weave in yarn ends. Soak or steam block according to the yarn care instructions, then dry flat.

## Tips
- Make a small gauge swatch before beginning if size matters.
- Try the strip around your neck before seaming.

## Common mistakes
- Tight cast-on or bind-off edges can pull in. Use a larger needle for those edges if needed.
- Check that the strip is not twisted before seaming.

## FAQ
### Can I make it taller?
Cast on more stitches in a multiple of four.

### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes. Check gauge and adjust the number of stitches to reach the desired height.
