This cheerful little coaster brings a handmade touch to a coffee table. Work a flat berry shape first, then add leaves at the top. This is an editorial draft awaiting stitch-count and usability review.

## At a glance
- Skill level: confident beginner
- Finished size: approximately 11 cm wide, depending on gauge
- Abbreviations: ch = chain, sc = single crochet, sl st = slip stitch

## Materials
- Cotton yarn in berry pink or red, approximately 25 g
- Small amount of green cotton yarn
- Tapestry needle

## Tools
- 3.5 mm crochet hook, or a size that makes a firm fabric
- Scissors
- Stitch marker

## Instructions
### 1. Make the berry base
With berry yarn, ch 13. Starting in the second chain from the hook, work 12 sc. Ch 1 and turn. Continue in rows, adding one sc at each end of the next three rows to widen the berry. Work four rows even, then decrease one stitch at each end of the following three rows. Fasten off.

### 2. Soften the edge
Join berry yarn at one upper corner. Work sc evenly around the shape. At the lower point, place three sc in one stitch to keep the corner smooth. Join with a sl st and fasten off.

### 3. Add leaves
Join green yarn along the top edge. For each leaf, ch 5, work back along the chain with sl st, sc, half-double crochet, and sl st, then anchor to the edge. Repeat three times, spacing the leaves evenly.

### 4. Finish
Weave in all ends. Dampen lightly and lay flat to dry. Keep the fabric firm enough to hold a cup securely.

## Tips
- Cotton yarn absorbs condensation better than acrylic for a coaster.
- If the piece curls, try a slightly larger hook or a gentle block.

## Common mistakes
- Uneven edge stitches can make the berry look lopsided. Mark both ends of each row.
- Loose finishing knots may show through. Weave yarn tails in two directions.

## FAQ
### Can I wash it?
Check the yarn label. Most cotton yarns can be washed gently and dried flat.

### Can I use another yarn weight?
Yes, though the coaster size and hook size will change.
