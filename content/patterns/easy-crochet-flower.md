This little flower is a friendly way to practice working in the round. Add it to a gift wrap, a bag, or another handmade project. This is demo editorial content: a human maker must check the stitch counts and finished result before publication. Instructions use US crochet terms.

## At a glance
- Skill level: beginner
- Finished size: approximately 6 cm across with the suggested yarn
- Abbreviations: ch = chain, sc = single crochet, dc = double crochet, sl st = slip stitch

## Materials
- Small amount of yellow cotton yarn for the center
- Small amount of pink cotton yarn for the petals
- Optional brooch pin or sewing thread for attaching the flower

## Tools
- 3.5 mm crochet hook
- Tapestry needle
- Scissors

## Instructions
### 1. Make the center
With yellow yarn, make a magic ring. Work 10 sc into the ring, pull it closed, and join to the first sc with a sl st. You should have 10 center stitches.

### 2. Add five petals
Join pink yarn. For each petal, work a sl st in the next center stitch. In the following center stitch, work ch 2, 2 dc, ch 2, and a sl st. Repeat this two-stitch sequence five times around the center.

### 3. Finish the flower
Fasten off and weave in both yarn tails on the back. Lay the petals flat with your fingers; lightly dampen and dry flat if you want a neater shape.

## Tips
- Count the 10 center stitches before changing color: each petal uses two of them.
- Keep the petal chains relaxed so the edges can spread into a rounded shape.
- Try the flower in a single color once the two-color version feels comfortable.

## Common mistakes
- A loose magic ring leaves a hole in the middle. Pull the tail firmly, then weave it in securely.
- Skipping a center stitch can leave you with only four petals. Place a marker in the first center stitch to track your starting point.

## FAQ
### Can I use acrylic yarn?
Yes. Choose a hook that makes a firm fabric and expect a slightly different size and drape.

### How do I attach the flower?
Use the yarn tails or matching sewing thread to stitch through the center and onto another item.
