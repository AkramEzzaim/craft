Preserve little pieces of a garden in a bookmark you can keep or give. This is an editorial draft awaiting materials and durability review.

## At a glance
- Skill level: beginner
- Active time: approximately 45 minutes
- Drying time: varies by adhesive and flowers

## Materials
- Fully dried pressed flowers and leaves
- Heavy cream cardstock or handmade paper
- Clear adhesive film or archival paper-safe glue
- Thin cotton cord or ribbon

## Tools
- Scissors or craft knife
- Ruler
- Hole punch
- Tweezers

## Instructions
### 1. Prepare the base
Cut cardstock into strips about 5 cm by 15 cm. Round corners if you like, and test one strip inside a book.

### 2. Arrange the flowers
Place dried flowers on each strip with tweezers. Leave a small margin around the edges and keep the upper hole area clear.

### 3. Seal the design
Fix the flowers with tiny dabs of paper-safe glue, then cover with clear film. Smooth from the center outward to release air bubbles. Trim any overhang.

### 4. Add the finishing touch
Punch a hole near the top and thread a short length of cotton cord. Let adhesive dry completely before using.

## Tips
- Flowers must be fully dry to avoid discoloration beneath the film.
- Use a light hand with glue so the paper does not buckle.

## Common mistakes
- Fresh flowers can trap moisture and brown over time.
- Film applied too quickly can create wrinkles. Smooth it slowly from one end.

## FAQ
### How long do the flowers need to press?
Many thin flowers press in one to two weeks. Thick flowers can take longer.

### Can children make these?
Yes, with adult help for sharp tools and adhesive film.
